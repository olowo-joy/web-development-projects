About

In 2023, two young women with big dreams and green thumbs decided Houston needed a little more color. What started as late-night conversations about creativity, friendship, and the simple joy of fresh flowers soon blossomed into a cozy little shop tucked away in Aliana, Texas. Their vision was simple: to bring a bit of freshness—literal and emotional—to their community. Surrounded by the hum of local life, they filled their small space with hand-tied bouquets, seasonal blooms, and the scent of something hopeful.

Every arrangement became more than flowers—it was a story of love, gratitude, celebration, and care. From birthdays to weddings to just-because moments, their creations became part of Houston's everyday beauty. Today, their flower shop continues to bloom as a place where artistry meets authenticity—a reminder that even in a bustling city, there's always room to slow down, breathe, and enjoy something fresh.
